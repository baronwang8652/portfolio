How I work

Systems before screens

I started as a system analyst, and it stuck. Before I open a layout file I want the flow, the roles and the states written down — partly because engineers can build from that, and partly because it is where the hard questions surface early instead of late.

The failure path is the design

Retry limits, lockouts, pending states, who gets notified when something stalls. Happy paths are the easy half. What a product does when things go wrong is what people actually remember about it.

Constraints are the material

Legal immutability, a third-party API that won't bend, plan tiers, a deadline. I'd rather work the real constraint honestly than design the version that could only exist if it weren't there.
